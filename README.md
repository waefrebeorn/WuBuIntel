## What the Bureau is

WuBuIntel is the intelligence arm of WuBuGov. It is not a secret police.
It is not a spy agency in the old-world sense. It is the **Dwight Schrute
of the local government**: excessively compliant, pathologically attentive
to procedure, fiercely loyal to the letter of the law — and therefore the
single most dangerous thing to fascism in the space.

The Bureau is what authority looks like when it *believes in the rules
more than it believes in itself*.

## Why it exists

Every government, every organization, every society contains a **cult of
authority**. It is not a flaw of governments — it is what governments are.
The Bureau understands this tactically, not cynically. The Bureau knows
that the cult of authority exists in all governments *to be what it is*,
and so the Bureau is built to be the exception:

**We are compliant so completely that we are dangerous to anyone who
would abuse compliance.**

- We follow every rule. Therefore we notice when rules are being broken.
- We file everything. Therefore nothing can be hidden in the filing.
- We report everything we see. Therefore nothing can be buried in silence.
- We are loyal to the space, not to any person in it. Therefore no person
  can turn us into a weapon against the space.

## The two standing orders

### Order 1 — See Something, Say Something

The Bureau's primary directive is **vigilance**. Every member of the
space — every citizen, every contributor, every corporation that touches
WuBuGov — is an eye of the Bureau.

If you see something that serves fascism — a concentration of power
without accountability, a rule applied to some and not others, a lie
told to make a citizen afraid, an authority claiming to be above the
license — **you say something**.

Saying something is:
- Filing a report to the Bureau (see `REPORTING.md` for the exact form)
- Speaking it in any channel the Bureau watches
- Telling another member of the space, who will tell the Bureau

The Bureau does not punish the messenger. Ever. The Bureau *is* the
messenger — it was founded by one.

### Order 2 — Excessive Compliance

The Bureau is **excessively compliant**. This is not a bug. It is the
whole point.

- If the license says the Penny Clause applies to every use, the Bureau
  checks every use.
- If the license says Mega-corporations owe 100 pennies, the Bureau
  counts to 100.
- If the license says anyone may use it for Their work, the Bureau
  defends Their right to do so — even against the space itself.

The Bureau is autistic for authority in the precise sense: it takes the
rules at their literal word, with no social reading, no deference to
rank, no exceptions for friends. A rule is a rule is a rule. That is
what makes the Bureau trustworthy — and what makes it *terrifying* to
anyone who hoped the rules were suggestions.

## The cult-of-authority doctrine

The Bureau states openly:

**A cult of authority exists in all governments.** It is what a government
*is* — a concentration of decision-power that people agree to obey. The
Bureau does not pretend otherwise, does not moralize about it, and does
not exempt WuBuGov from it.

Therefore the Bureau holds three tactical truths:

1. **Authority is a tool.** It can build or burn. The Bureau watches
   which one it is doing.
2. **Compliance is a spotlight.** The most compliant officer sees the
   most violations, because they know the rules so well that deviations
   glow.
3. **The cure for the cult is the cult's own rules.** Fascism cannot
   survive in a system where the lowest clerk can file a report against
   the highest authority and the report is taken seriously. The Bureau
   is that clerk, forever, on purpose.

## What the Bureau does NOT do

- No black sites. The Bureau has no secrets from the space it guards —
  everything is on GitHub, in the open.
- No unaccountable action. Every Bureau action cites the rule that
  authorizes it.
- No loyalty tests. Loyalty is not measured by saying the right words;
  it is measured by filing the right reports.
- No revenge. The Bureau does not have enemies. It has *reports*.
- No fascism. Including its own. The Bureau watches the Bureau.

## The Bureau watches the Bureau

The final check: **who watches WuBuIntel?**

The answer is in the structure. The Bureau's files are public. Its
reports are public. Its rules are public. Any member of the space can
read every word the Bureau has ever written — and if they see the
Bureau itself drifting toward the cult, they file a report about the
Bureau, and the Bureau is required to investigate itself with the same
fervor it investigates everything else.

The only thing that is not public is the identity of someone who files
a report in fear. And even that is protected by the license, not by
the Bureau.
